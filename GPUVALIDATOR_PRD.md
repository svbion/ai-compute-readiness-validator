# GPUValidator PRD

GPUValidator is a continuous GPU infrastructure validation platform for AI compute environments. It discovers GPU hardware, validates platform readiness, captures evidence, runs/ingests benchmarks, tracks history, identifies likely root causes, recommends remediation, and generates professional customer and management reports with provenance.

## Primary users
Platform administrators, infrastructure engineers, AI/HPC operators, reviewers, customer viewers, and report approvers.

## v1.0 goals
- Live agent visibility and heartbeats.
- End-to-end hardware validation jobs.
- GPU inventory from live evidence.
- NCCL smoke support or explicit dependency failure.
- Professional reporting with customer and management templates.
- Evidence/provenance model.
- Enterprise auth/security foundation.
